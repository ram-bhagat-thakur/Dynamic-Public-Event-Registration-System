# 🌟 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to building a welcoming environment regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- 💬 Using welcoming and inclusive language
- 🧠 Giving thoughtful, constructive feedback
- 🙌 Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- 🧪 Gracefully accepting criticism and learning from it
- 🧩 Focusing on what’s best for the community and the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- 🚫 Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- 🚫 Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- 🚫 Publishing others’ private information without consent
- 🚫 Dismissive or hostile responses to accessibility or UX concerns

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

---

## Reporting Violations

Violations can be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers privately. All reports will be handled confidentially and respectfully.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and safety of reporters.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and external channels like LinkedIn or community forums — and applies when an individual is representing the project or its community.
